SpanishPal Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Overview

SpanishPal ("we," "us," "our") is an AI-powered Spanish conversation app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Voice and conversation data. When you use SpanishPal, you speak into your microphone. Depending on the feature you use, your voice is processed as follows:

Speak tab (live conversation with Elena): Your voice audio is streamed directly to OpenAI via a secure real-time connection. OpenAI handles transcription, response generation, and voice output in one step. Your audio and the resulting conversation text are processed by OpenAI's servers.

Lessons and Role Play tabs:

  1. Your voice audio is converted to text by Deepgram — a third-party speech-to-text service.
  2. The text is sent to OpenAI to generate Elena's responses.
  3. Elena's responses are converted to voice by ElevenLabs — a third-party text-to-speech service.

By using SpanishPal, you consent to your voice and conversation text being processed by these third-party AI services.

Account data. SpanishPal uses anonymous device identifiers — no email address or account is required. Anonymous session data is stored in Supabase, our backend provider.

Usage data. We collect anonymized usage events (screens viewed, features used) via PostHog to improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

Purchase data. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). We use RevenueCat to track subscription status. We never see your payment card details.

Third-Party AI Processing

SpanishPal's core feature — conversation with Elena — is powered by third-party AI services. Specifically:

  • OpenAI processes your voice audio and conversation text to generate Elena's responses and voice output. See OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com/privacy.
  • Deepgram processes your voice audio to produce text transcripts (Lessons and Role Play tabs). See Deepgram's privacy policy at deepgram.com/privacy.
  • ElevenLabs converts Elena's text responses to voice audio (Lessons and Role Play tabs). See ElevenLabs's privacy policy at elevenlabs.io/privacy.

Your conversations are processed in real time and are not used to train AI models unless you separately opt in to any such program offered by those providers.

Data Retention

  • Conversation history is stored to maintain context across sessions and personalize your experience. You can delete your conversation history by deleting the app.
  • To request deletion of all data associated with your device, email: support@migdiomedia.com

Children

SpanishPal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact: support@migdiomedia.com

Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notice.
